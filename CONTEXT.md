# Pinote

Small annotations attached to a location or a piece of interface content.

## Language

**Pinote**: An annotation consisting of a marker and the content it reveals.

**Trigger**: The interactive marker that reveals a pinote's content.

**Preview**: Content revealed temporarily by hovering or focusing a trigger.

**Open pinote**: Content kept visible after explicit activation, until dismissed.

**Expansion**: A presentation in which the marker becomes the content surface and returns to its original shape when closed.

**Attachment**: The text or interface element to which a pinote is anchored.

**Provider**: A group of pinotes that shares interaction settings and has at most one open pinote.

**Layer**: The coordinate area in which standalone pinotes are placed and moved.
